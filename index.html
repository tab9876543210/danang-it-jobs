Beyond these two frequently-listed names, most Đà Nẵng postings come from offshore-development houses serving Japanese, US and European clients rather than local product companies — worth knowing if you're optimizing for product vs. outsourcing work.

What Project Managers should be building toward

Vietnam's 2026 hiring data and global PM trend reports point the same direction: the pay ceiling is shifting from "runs Agile ceremonies well" toward roles that blend delivery with technical and business judgment.

AI-assisted planning (Jira/Asana AI, forecasting) Data-driven reporting over gut-feel status updates Hybrid Agile / Waterfall fluency Stakeholder & cross-team leadership Business-outcome framing, not just on-time/on-budget Japanese (N2+) for Đà Nẵng's BrSE-heavy market

Tech Lead and IT Manager already out-earn a pure Project Leader/PM track in the salary chart above, and Product Owner tops both — the market prices technical-plus-strategic hybrids higher than process management alone.
